This is a Discord bot I coded in a couple languages however I used primarily Python. I was experimenting with modding videogames and figured it would be funny if my friends could mod my videogame while I was playing it.

This bot can:
- read messages in discord and execute commands
- modify the environments in videogames (I coded it so I could have the specific game's mods go in and call my bot to commit an action)
- interact with users
  - the bot can provide trivia questions that will play sound effects based off of the answer provided
  - the bot can "haunt" people in discord by entering calls that have people in them and playing random sound effects such as simple as sound files or even youtube videos

It is called a ping pong bot because I was testing a basic call and response command while learning how to make the bot interact with messages. I would type "!ping" and it replies with "pong". The name stuck.
