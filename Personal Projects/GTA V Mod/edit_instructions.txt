=====================================================
 GTA V MOD - BUILD & UPDATE INSTRUCTIONS
=====================================================

1. Make your changes to your C# files (e.g., adding effects, updating code).
2. Save your files (Ctrl + S).
3. Open your terminal inside the "GTA V Mod" folder.
4. Run this exact copy-paste command to build and update your game:

   dotnet build GtaModProject.csproj; copy bin\Debug\net48\GtaModProject.dll "D:\Grand Theft Auto V Legacy\scripts\"

5. Launch GTA V and enjoy!
